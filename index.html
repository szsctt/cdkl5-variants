About this resource

Variant names follow HGVS protein nomenclature on isoform (). Predicted effects are worked out from the variant name only and do not account for nonsense-mediated decay or alternative splicing.

The 3D view uses the crystal structure (residues ), displayed with PDBe Mol* (Sehnal et al., Nucleic Acids Research 2021). The C-terminal region is disordered and has no experimental structure.

For research and education only. Not intended for clinical diagnosis or variant interpretation.
